Pragya Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Pragya is a client-side reading tracker and commonplace book web application. Your reading data belongs exclusively to you and is stored directly in your own Google account (or locally in your browser when using Demo Mode).

1. Data We Access & How It Is Used

When you sign in with Google, Pragya requests the following Google API scopes:

  • email and profile: Used to display your signed-in account inside the app and isolate local browser caches when switching accounts.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets: Used to create and synchronize your personal Pragya Commonplace spreadsheet (books, notes, reading sessions, and behavioral delta logs).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Used to create and update your Pragya Commonplace spreadsheet and store photo attachments you upload in your Pragya Media/ folder.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly: Used strictly in read-only mode to check your spreadsheet's modifiedTime for fast reloads, render private images in Pragya Media/, and resolve titles for Google Docs you attach to notes.

2. Data Storage & Zero-Backend Architecture

  • No application database: Pragya does not run a database or collect, store, sell, or share your books, notes, images, or Google account information.
  • Direct browser-to-Google sync: All Google Sheets and Google Drive API requests travel directly from your browser to Google APIs. Local caching uses your browser's IndexedDB, localStorage, and Cache API solely for offline read-only resilience and fast reloads.
  • Optional stateless OAuth refresh proxy: If an Auth Proxy Worker is enabled to keep mobile PWA sessions signed in, it operates strictly statelessly with zero storage or logging of user data. It only exchanges/refreshes Google OAuth tokens and returns an AES-256-GCM sealed refresh token stored solely on your device.
  • Zero AI data sharing: The Pragya web application makes zero third-party AI/LLM calls and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Pragya's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Deleting Data & Revoking Access

  • Click Log out inside Pragya to revoke your active session token and clear cached workspace data on your device.
  • You can revoke Pragya's Google permissions at any time from your Google Account Permissions page, and delete the Pragya Commonplace Sheet and Pragya Media/ folder directly from your Google Drive.

5. Contact

Questions or issues can be reported via the open-source repository at github.com/saimanoj01/pragya.
